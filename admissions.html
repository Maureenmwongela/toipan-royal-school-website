JOIN TOIPAN ROYAL GIRLS HIGH SCHOOL

We welcome students seeking quality education in a disciplined, supportive and nurturing environment. Admissions are open for the following classes:

Grade 7

We offer quality education guided by the Competency-Based Education (CBE) approach. Our trained and dedicated teachers support every learner to build strong academic foundations. We also take learner projects seriously, helping girls develop practical skills, creativity and confidence as they prepare for the next level of learning.

Grade 8

Grade 8 learners receive quality CBE-focused education from trained and dedicated teachers. We strengthen academic knowledge while giving learners opportunities to participate in projects, practical activities and teamwork that build skills needed for their continued learning journey.

Grade 9

We prepare our Grade 9 learners for the next stage of their education through quality teaching, practical learning and meaningful projects. Our trained teachers guide every girl to develop confidence, responsibility, creativity and the competencies needed for progression to senior school.

Grade 10

Grade 10 learners are guided through the CBE senior school pathway with quality teaching and close academic support. We encourage practical learning, projects, creativity and career awareness while helping every girl develop the skills and confidence required for the next stage of education.

Grade 11

Grade 11 learners continue with the CBE senior school pathway through quality teaching, practical learning and meaningful projects. Our trained teachers provide close academic guidance while helping girls develop competencies, confidence, creativity and career awareness as they prepare for the next stage of their education.

Form 4

Form 4 learners receive focused preparation for the KCSE examination. Through experienced teachers, regular revision, assessments, academic guidance and dedicated support, we prepare our candidates adequately to approach their KCSE examinations with confidence.
